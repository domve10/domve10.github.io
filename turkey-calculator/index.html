Thawing chart (in the fridge, 40°F / 4°C or below)

WeightFridgeCold water*
8–12 lb (3.6–5.4 kg)1–3 days4–6 h
12–16 lb (5.4–7.3 kg)3–4 days6–8 h
16–20 lb (7.3–9.1 kg)4–5 days8–10 h
20–24 lb (9.1–11 kg)5–6 days10–12 h

Fridge: about 24 hours for every 4–5 lb. *Cold water: about 30 minutes per lb, bird in a leak-proof bag, water changed every 30 minutes, then cook straight away. Source: USDA FSIS, Let's Talk Turkey. The calculator uses the safer 4 lb per day.

Roasting times, unstuffed, at 325°F / 163°C

WeightApprox. time
8–12 lb2¾–3 h
12–14 lb3–3¾ h
14–18 lb3¾–4¼ h
18–20 lb4¼–4½ h
20–24 lb4½–5 h

Times are a guide: the turkey is done at 165°F / 74°C in the thickest part of the thigh, the wing and the breast (USDA). Use a probe thermometer and start checking 30 minutes early. Rest 30–45 minutes under loose foil; it stays hot, and it frees the oven for the sides.

Chef's tips

  • Above about 20 guests, roast two smaller birds or add a turkey breast: they cook faster and more evenly.
  • Dry-brine the day before (about 1% of the weight in fine salt, uncovered in the fridge) for seasoned meat and crisp skin.
  • Make the gravy base from the neck and giblets on Wednesday, finish it with the roasting juices while the bird rests.

FAQ

What size turkey do I need per person?

The USDA guideline is 1 lb of whole turkey per person. As a chef I plan about 1¼ lb (570 g) of whole, bone-in turkey per adult for a generous dinner with some leftovers, or 1½ lb (680 g) for plenty of leftovers.

When should I start thawing my turkey for Thanksgiving?

In the fridge, allow about one day for every 4–5 lb. For Thanksgiving on Thursday 26 November 2026, a 16 lb bird should go into the fridge by Saturday 21 or Sunday 22 November.

How long does a turkey take to roast?

Unstuffed at 325°F / 163°C, about 3–3¾ hours for 12–14 lb and 3¾–4¼ hours for 14–18 lb (USDA). It is done at 165°F / 74°C in the thickest part of the thigh, wing and breast.

Can I thaw a turkey in cold water?

Yes: about 30 minutes per lb, in a leak-proof bag, changing the water every 30 minutes, and cook it immediately afterwards (USDA).
